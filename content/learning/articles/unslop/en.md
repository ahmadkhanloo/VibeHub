# Unslop: human writing instead of robotic tone

AI text carries tell-tale patterns: "Here's the thing:", rhyming pairs, grandiose intros. Unslop finds and removes those patterns so the text reads like a human wrote it.

## What it gives you

- **Audit mode:** flag AI patterns in the text without changing a word.
- **Two-pass rewrite:** diagnose first, then reconstruct the text while keeping meaning and a genuine voice.
- **A catalogue of known tells:** stock phrases, formulaic structures and overused words.

## When to use it

Before publishing anything AI wrote: docs, READMEs, user-facing messages, blog posts. Say "make it sound human" or "unslop".

## Install state

The workspace chip reports whether Unslop is present among the agent's local skills.
