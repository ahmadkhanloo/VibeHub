# Stageshow: faux product demos and illustrations

Stageshow builds product UI that looks real — as one still illustration or a short animated demo with a cursor. It frames, masks and crops browser and app windows, dashboards, phones, notifications, terminals and bento grids the way a marketing page, feature card or docs hero needs.

## What it gives you

- **A portable React kit:** ready parts like `Stage`, `Window`, `Phone` and the `Act` animation API, with no third-party animation runtime.
- **Authored demos, not browser automation:** `a.click()` draws the cursor pressing and `a.set()` changes the scene; every motion has a cause.
- **Rehearsal gate:** the filming script's `--gate` prints a contact sheet plus a verdict, and hand-over only counts after "rehearsal passed".
- **Gold examples and bad/good pairs:** start from the quality bar in `examples/` and diagnose faults with the teaching pairs in `teaching/`.

## When to use it

Whenever a faux UI is needed: a hero or feature-card illustration in the style of a polished marketing site, an animated-cursor product walkthrough, or teaching scenes about an interface. Just ask the agent to build the fake UI or product demo with this skill.

## Install state

The workspace chip reports whether Stageshow is present among the agent's local skills.
